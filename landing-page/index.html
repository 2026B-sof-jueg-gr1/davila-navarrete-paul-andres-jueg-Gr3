// The script

How the night goes

  1. 01

    Slide

    Arrows or WASD move the killer, and he does not stop until he hits something. Plan the path, use the furniture, and take out every victim before you run out of moves.

  2. 02

    Hunt the final girl

    With the board cleared, the last victim shows up. You only get a few moves to corner her before she escapes the theater.

  3. 03

    Final blow

    Stop the needle in the green zone three times in a row. The zone shrinks every round, and on Hard it barely exists.

  4. 04

    Listen to Mommy

    In World 02, press Space and Jason's mother marks the next five moves of the best route. Three uses per level, so save them.
